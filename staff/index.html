Staff (admin) / स्टाफ

Leave the temporary password blank and the staff member's mobile number is the temporary password. At the first login the staff member must verify the email code and set a new password.

After adding, tell the staff member to log in with their email and the temporary password (or their mobile number if you left it blank). They get an emailed code, then choose a new password. The Invite button is the older route (8-digit code handed over in person) and still works.

Master password (admin only)

Digits only. It is stored as a salted hash and cannot be read back. Use a number that is not a phone number, date or pattern.
