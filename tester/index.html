How a testing session works

  1. The developer (Deepak Kushwaha) shares this page’s link in the tester’s chat, along with the starter message below.
  2. The tester opens this page and reads the rules and the current task list.
  3. The tester performs the task and writes a complete report: what was tested, what passed, what failed, and exact steps to reproduce every issue.
  4. The report is submitted through the form at the bottom of this page. AI chat assistants cannot click or submit web forms on their own, so the developer pastes the tester’s report text into the form. This takes under a minute.
  5. The report reaches the team by email and the tester is told the result in the next chat session.
